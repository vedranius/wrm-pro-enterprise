# Institution

This document records how the project becomes sustainable beyond any single person or company.
It is the governance of last resort: the goal is that WRM outlives its founder.

## The two options

| Option | Strengths | Drawbacks |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** (Apache Software Foundation, CNCF, Software Freedom Conservancy) | neutral trademark, established governance, legal protection, sponsorships | slower decisions, cost, loss of control |
| **Irrevocable guarantee + independent board** | speed, keeps direction | less trust than a foundation; still depends on people |

**Recommendation:** evaluate both during Phase 6 and decide no later than month 18. If the
project gains five or more corporate users, a foundation is probably the right answer.

## Elements

- **Irrevocable licence guarantee** — a published release stays under the licence it was
  published with, forever. Past releases are never retroactively re-licensed (see
  [LICENSE-CHANGE.md](../LICENSE-CHANGE.md)).
- **Independent security board** — two external members who review critical fixes.
- **Annual external audit** and public postmortems.
- **Trademark transfer** or a clearly published usage policy (see [TRADEMARK.md](../TRADEMARK.md)).
- **Public vulnerability statistics** — count, severity, and time-to-fix (see
  [security-statistics.md](security-statistics.md)).
